# Semantic & Embedding-Guided Segmentation

- Use embeddings and topic segmentation to form semantically coherent chunks.

## Details

Discuss algorithms and practical tips:

- Embedding similarity clustering (agglomerative, k-means, spectral) to group contiguous text.
- Topic segmentation (TextTiling, transformer-based change point detection).
- Balancing semantic coherence with retrieval performance and index size.
- Pitfalls: embedding drift, computation cost, and cross-document coherence.

Lab: segment a long article using embeddings and compare against heading-based segmentation.
