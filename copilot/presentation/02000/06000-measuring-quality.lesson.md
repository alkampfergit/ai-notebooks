# Measuring Chunk Quality

- Metrics and experiments to evaluate chunking choices.

## Details

Topics and methods:

- Intrinsic metrics: coherence (topic purity), chunk size distribution, overlap redundancy.
- Extrinsic metrics: retrieval recall/precision, downstream QA accuracy, generation faithfulness.
- A/B testing and offline evaluation: simulation with holdout queries and human labels.
- Instrumentation: logging retrieval traces and cost telemetry.

Practical: build a small benchmark and compute recall/precision for different chunkers.
