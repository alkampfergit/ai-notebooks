# Semantic Enrichment & Annotation

Add machine-readable meaning to boost retrieval precision & answer grounding.

## Details

Focus on entity recognition, linking to knowledge bases, key phrase & topic tagging, summarization, embedding generation strategies, relationship extraction, and constructing light-weight knowledge graphs. Include quality evaluation (precision/recall trade-offs, human review loops) and cost optimization.

### Outcomes
* Apply NER & entity linking for disambiguation.
* Generate multi-vector representations (dense, sparse, hybrid).
* Derive relations/facts with provenance.
* Evaluate enrichment impact on retrieval KPIs.
