# Chunking Strategies

Design chunks that maximize retrieval signal while preserving semantic integrity.

## Details

Focus on methods for partitioning raw extracted content into retrievable units. Compare fixed window, sliding, structural (headings / DOM / schema), semantic (embedding similarity / topic segmentation), and hybrid adaptive approaches. Discuss evaluation of chunk granularity on latency, recall, precision, and answer coherence.

### Outcomes
* Differentiate mechanical vs. semantic chunking.
* Select chunk size & overlap heuristics given token budgets & domain structure.
* Apply adaptive chunking for mixed structured/unstructured corpora.
* Instrument retrieval quality impacts of alternative chunk pipelines.
