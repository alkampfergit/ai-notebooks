# Quality, Validation & Pitfalls

Institutionalize reliability: testing, monitoring, and anti-pattern avoidance.

## Details

Consolidates cross-cutting concerns: data quality metrics, validation harnesses, drift detection, PII handling, observability (latency, coverage, freshness), and common failure modes (over-chunking, metadata loss, silent extraction errors). Provides a readiness checklist and governance templates.

### Outcomes
* Define and instrument extraction quality KPIs.
* Detect and remediate drift / degradation.
* Implement guardrails for compliance & privacy.
* Avoid recurring design & operational pitfalls.
